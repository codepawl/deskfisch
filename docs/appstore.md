**Description**
Deskfisch is a pixel-art aquarium you look after, not a screensaver.

You start with an empty tank. Pour sand, plant something, fill it with water, then wait for the
nitrogen cycle: ammonia, nitrite, nitrate. A test kit shows you where it stands. Fish added too
early get stressed, so the first days are about patience.

Eight species, each with its own pace and gait. Danios dart, angelfish glide, corys hop along
the sand. Some come for every flake, some turn their nose up. New fish arrive in a bag: float it,
add tank water a few times, then drag it under.

Care is hands-on. Feed by pinching flakes onto the surface, scrape algae off the glass, siphon
waste from the sand, change water with a conditioner. Skip it and the water tells you.

Pick a fish up and put it down somewhere else. Not realistic. Very fun.

Slow chiptune music, off by default. A day and night that follow your clock. Three play
styles, from zen (nothing dies) to hardcore.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your tank is saved on your device. Also on desktop and in the
browser at deskfisch.codepawl.com.
