What makes it different

A real nitrogen cycle

Ammonia becomes nitrite becomes nitrate. A fresh tank takes days to cycle, and fish added too early get stressed. Buy a test kit to see it happen.

Pet mode

A borderless, see-through window that floats above your apps. Pin it in place or drag it anywhere. Chill mode hides every control.

Acclimation that matters

New fish arrive in a bag. Float it, mix in tank water, then drag it under the surface. Rush it and the fish is shocked.

Care, not clicking

Feed by hand, scrub algae, vacuum the gravel, change water with conditioner. Skip it and you will see ich and fin rot.

Breeding and a shop

Happy guppies and mollies drop fry that grow into sellable adults. Coins buy filters, heaters, lights, bigger tanks and decor.

Follows the sun

Without a tank light the room goes dark at night and rose-gold at dawn and dusk, on your real clock or the tank's own simulated day.

How a tank starts

  1. Buy a filter and a heater. The filter is where the good bacteria live.
  2. Drop a pinch of food into the empty tank. It rots into ammonia, which feeds the bacteria.
  3. Watch the cycle. Ammonia spikes, then nitrite, then both fall. A few real days.
  4. Buy your first fish. Schooling species need a group of their own kind.
  5. Acclimate and release. Float the bag ten minutes, add tank water three times, drag it under.
  6. Feed once or twice a day, change some water weekly. That is the whole hobby.
