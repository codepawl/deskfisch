What makes it different

A real nitrogen cycle

Ammonia becomes nitrite becomes nitrate. A fresh tank takes days to cycle, and fish added too early get stressed. Buy a test kit to see it happen.

Pet mode

A borderless, see-through window that floats above your apps. Pin it in place or drag it anywhere. Chill mode hides every control.

Acclimation that matters

New fish arrive in a bag. Float it, mix in tank water, then drag it under the surface. Rush it and the fish is shocked.

Care, not clicking

Feed by hand, scrub algae, vacuum the gravel, change water with conditioner. Skip it and you will see ich and fin rot.

Breeding and a shop

Happy guppies and mollies drop fry that grow into sellable adults. Coins buy filters, heaters, lights, bigger tanks and decor.

Follows the sun

Without a tank light the room goes dark at night and rose-gold at dawn and dusk, on your real clock or the tank's own simulated day.

How a tank starts

  1. Pour in some sandFish and plants need a bed to live on. Plant something while it is dry.
  2. Fill it with tap water, then add a filter and a heaterConditioner keeps the chlorine out. The filter is where the good bacteria live.
  3. Drop a pinch of food and watch the cycleIt rots into ammonia, which feeds the bacteria. Ammonia spikes, then nitrite, then both fall. A few real days.
  4. Buy your first fishSchooling species need a group of their own kind.
  5. Acclimate and releaseFloat the bag ten minutes, add tank water three times, drag it under.
  6. Feed once or twice a day, change some water weeklyThat is the whole hobby.

Questions

macOS says it could not verify the app, or that it may be malware.
The build is not notarized yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Close that box, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “Deskfisch was blocked” and click Open Anyway. On older macOS, right-click the app and choose Open. If it still refuses, run this once in Terminal and try again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Deskfisch.app
Windows shows a blue SmartScreen box.
Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows has no publisher to show. The download is exactly what GitHub Actions built from the public source.
Does it collect anything?
No. The app has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. The desktop app makes one kind of network request: an update check against GitHub Releases. The website reads the release list from the GitHub API and counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track individual visitors.
Where is my tank saved?
In your user data folder, with the last five saves backed up before each launch. Settings → Save file exports it as JSON and imports it on another machine. The browser version saves in that browser only.
Pet mode is not staying on top on Linux.
On GNOME Wayland the app runs through XWayland so always-on-top works. Some other compositors ignore it; window mode still works there.
Can I leave it running all day?
That is the idea. At the default 30 fps it uses about a fifth of one core, less while unfocused, and the tank keeps living while the app is closed, catching up when you come back.
